## ARR cycles and "under review at ARR"

- Oct 2026 cycle: submission Oct 12, 2026; reviews and meta-reviews "TBA"; cycle end and the
  NAACL/COLING commitment Dec 23, 2026. Jan 2027 cycle: "January, 2027", day not yet announced;
  feeds ACL 2027 ([ARR dates](https://aclrollingreview.org/dates)). No November or December cycle
  exists (the 2026 cadence: Jan 5, Mar 16, May 25, Aug 3, Oct 12):
  [ARR incentives 2026](http://aclrollingreview.org/incentives2026).
- CV meaning: a paper "is considered under review by ARR" from submission until the meta-review
  arrives; after that it is not under review anywhere until committed to a conference, which
  then decides ([ARR authors](https://aclrollingreview.org/authors)). The honest CV line is
  "Under review, ACL Rolling Review (Jan 2027 cycle), to be committed to ACL 2027", and
  non-anonymous arXiv posting is permitted at any time ([ARR anonymity](http://aclrollingreview.org/anonymity/)).

## Journals

- JOSS: open review in public GitHub issues; software only ("pre-trained ML models and notebooks
  are not in-scope"); the repo must have been public with active development for more than six
  months before submission; first-year median 32 days from submission to publication (2016-17
  data): [about](https://joss.theoj.org/about), [submitting](https://joss.readthedocs.io/en/latest/submitting.html),
  [PeerJ CS paper](https://peerj.com/articles/cs-147/).
- Scientific Data (Data Descriptors): median 11 days to first editorial decision, 162 days to
  acceptance (2025): [metrics](https://www.nature.com/sdata/journal-impact).
- PeerJ Computer Science: "first decision in 35 days"; optional published review history; APC
  $2,155: [overview](https://peerj.com/computer-science/overview-and-features/).
- Data in Brief: the Elsevier pages returned 403; a third-party listing reports about 7 days to
  first decision, unverified: [journal page](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/data-in-brief).

## arXiv endorsement

No deadline, but a changed rule. Policy effective Jan 21, 2026: automatic endorsement needs BOTH
an institutional email AND a prior arXiv paper in the same endorsement domain; otherwise a
personal endorsement from an established author in that domain (a cs.CL endorsement does not
carry to cs.IR or cs.DB): [arXiv blog](https://blog.arxiv.org/2026/01/21/attention-authors-updated-endorsement-policy/).
With no prior arXiv paper, an endorser has to be lined up before Nov 16.

## Recommendation: three targets

1. **ECIR 2027 Resource track, Nov 2, 2026** (notification Dec 7, before every application
   deadline). Best fit: single-blind, arXiv allowed, 12 pages, Springer. Must be true by Nov 2:
   build stop pulled forward to about Oct 25; GraphRAG-Bench and LongMemEval numbers final;
   the Kaggle dataset and code public under a license; the paper complete two weeks ahead of the
   planned Nov 15 freeze; a travel budget for Southampton in March.
2. **PVLDB Vol. 20, Dec 1, 2026 (abstract Nov 25), Experiment, Analysis and Benchmark
   category**: fits the Nov 15 freeze exactly; initial reviews about Jan 15, 2027, so "under
   review at PVLDB" is true on Dec 1 and a decision may land before Jan 15. Must be true: a
   12-page VLDB-format paper framed as measured experiments (costs, latencies, benchmark numbers),
   not novelty; a revision is likely, so the system stays runnable into spring.
3. **A matching EACL 2027 workshop, direct submission Dec 15, 2026** (list published Oct 2;
   notification Jan 5, 2027; archival in the ACL Anthology if the workshop chooses): the
   peer-reviewed safety net if 1 or 2 slips; AAAI-27 workshops (Nov 20, notification Dec 2) are
   the earlier but non-archival alternative. Must be true: a KG, RAG or agent-memory workshop on
   the Oct 2 list; an 8-page ACL-format version of the same paper.

Skip the ARR Oct 12 cycle (before the draft exists, and it needs a qualified co-author); the Jan
2027 ARR cycle only helps if its day falls before about Jan 15.
