## Headline: the project is now ThreadAtlas

As I ingested representative documents and watched what the pipeline actually produces, the center
of gravity moved from a ledger of isolated facts to ordered, entity-specific narrative cells. I
renamed the system **ThreadAtlas**: a thread is an entity's ordered sequence of narrative cells as
it passes through documents; the atlas is the navigable organization that forms when shared
entities connect works. FactLedger is kept as the name of the internal evidence subsystem (facts,
verbatim quotes, temporal validity, provenance). Facts and quotes remain as evidence and temporal
machinery, but narrative cells are now the primary reading and retrieval representation, and the
wiki becomes a generated read model over the graph rather than a second database.

there is nothing to host; retrieval uses brute-force search, so it needs no database extensions.
