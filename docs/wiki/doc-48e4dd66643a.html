The Marvelous Land of Oz

L. Frank Baum, 1904-06
Part of Works around L. Frank Baum

The Marvelous Land of Oz is a fantasy sequel introduced by L. Frank Baum as a response to children who wanted more of the Scarecrow and Tin Woodman; it is dedicated to David C. Montgomery and Frank A. Stone. It begins with Tip, a youth mistreated by the sorceress Mombi in the Country of the Gillikins. Tip creates Jack Pumpkinhead to frighten her, but Mombi brings Jack to life with the Powder of Life and plans to turn Tip into marble. Tip escapes with Jack and the powder, animates a wooden Saw-Horse, and heads for the Emerald City. Jack reaches the Scarecrow, ruler of the Emerald City, while Tip encounters General Jinjur’s all-girl Army of Revolt. Jinjur’s soldiers seize the city without bloodshed, forcing Tip, the Scarecrow, Jack, and the Saw-Horse to flee. They seek aid from the Tin Woodman, now nickel-plated Emperor of the Winkies, and are joined by the highly educated Woggle-Bug. Mombi, aiding Jinjur, blocks their return with illusions, but they reach the palace. The Scarecrow’s hidden field mice frighten the rebels away, though the friends remain trapped by Jinjur’s surrounding army. Using Tip’s remaining Powder of Life, the party animates a winged machine with a Gump’s head and escapes. After crashing in a Jackdaw’s Nest, they survive attacks, repair the Gump with a wishing pill, and fly to Glinda the Good. Glinda says the true ruler is Ozma, daughter of King Pastoria, and suspects Mombi concealed her. Glinda’s army surrounds the Emerald City, captures Mombi despite her transformations, and compels her confession: the Wizard of Oz brought baby Ozma to her, and Mombi enchanted Ozma into Tip. Mombi restores Tip as Princess Ozma, the rightful ruler. Although Ozma fears her friends may no longer care for her, Glinda’s forces defeat Jinjur, whose army surrenders. Ozma takes the throne, restores order, and releases Jinjur on a promise of good behavior. The Gump is rewarded by being taken apart, while the Tin Woodman and Scarecrow return to the Winkies, where brains, heart, and contentment are valued above money.

The Marvelous Land of Oz

L. Frank Baum explains that he wrote The Marvelous Land of Oz after receiving many letters from children asking for more about the Scarecrow and Tin Woodman. He says he promised a girl named Dorothy that he would write the book after receiving a thousand such letters, and he now says he has kept that promise. The book is dedicated to David C. Montgomery and Frank A. Stone for their performances as the Tin Woodman and Scarecrow. Its chapter list names adventures involving Tip, Pumpkinhead, the Scarecrow, Gen. Jinjur, the Tin Woodman, Old Mombi, Glinda the Good, and Princess Ozma of Oz.

Tip Manufactures a Pumpkinhead

Tip, a youth raised by the sorceress Mombi, lives in the Country of the Gillikins and performs chores while finding ways to amuse himself. He carves a large pumpkin into a jolly face and builds a wooden human figure to wear it. Tip dresses the figure in clothing taken from Mombi’s chest and names it Jack Pumpkinhead. He plans to use the creation to frighten Mombi when she returns from the village.

The Marvelous Powder of Life

Tip places his wooden man, Jack, at a bend in the road to frighten Mombi, but Mombi uses a new Powder of Life to bring him to life. Jack Pumpkinhead begins speaking and walking, while Mombi locks him in a cow-stable stall and takes Tip into the house. Mombi prepares a mixture of milk, vinegar, herbs, and powders intended to transform Tip into a marble statue. Tip remains awake after Mombi locks the mixture in her room, fearing what will happen at daybreak.

The Flight of the Fugitives

Tip escapes from Mombi at night to avoid being turned into a marble statue, taking bread, cheese, and the Powder of Life. He returns to the stable and brings Jack Pumpkinhead with him, and the two travel south toward the Emerald City. Tip explains the history and rulers of the Land of Oz, including Dorothy, the Scarecrow, and the Tin Woodman. Jack accepts Tip as his creator and parent and agrees to follow him.

Tip Makes an Experiment in Magic

Tip and Jack travel together, but Jack’s wooden joints weaken when he tries to sit down. Tip explains the difference between a living horse and the wooden saw-horse they find at the edge of the wood. He wonders whether the Powder of Life can animate the saw-horse, and repeats the words and gestures he saw Mombi use. The saw-horse comes to life, astonishing Tip and pleasing Jack.

The Awakening of the Saw-horse

Tip brings the Saw-Horse to life, but the wooden creature initially cannot control its stiff legs or hear commands because it has no ears. Tip gives it bark ears, teaches it commands, and mounts Jack Pumpkinhead on its back for their journey to the Emerald City. The Saw-Horse learns to obey Tip, though it stumbles into a gopher-hole before being set upright. At night, nine miles from the Emerald City, Tip camps beside the road while the Saw-Horse stands watch.

Jack Pumpkinhead’s Ride to the Emerald City

Tip, Jack Pumpkinhead, and the Saw-Horse resume their journey to the Emerald City, but must cross a broad river without the ferryman’s help. After crossing, Tip accidentally commands the Saw-Horse to run too fast, loses his grip on its tail, and is left behind while Jack and the Saw-Horse continue to the city. Jack reaches the Emerald City, where the Guardian of the Gates and the Soldier with the Green Whiskers bring him to the palace to meet the Scarecrow. The Emerald City is described as a magnificent green city ruled by the Scarecrow.

His Majesty the Scarecrow

His Majesty the Scarecrow meets Jack Pumpkinhead, who is surprised by the Scarecrow’s straw-filled body and jeweled crown. Believing that they speak different languages because they come from different countries, the Scarecrow summons Jellia Jamb to act as an interpreter. Jellia reveals that the Munchkin and Gillikin languages are the same, and the Scarecrow and Jack become friends. The Scarecrow then takes Jack into the courtyard to play quoits.

Gen. Jinjur’s Army of Revolt

Tip meets General Jinjur, who commands an Army of Revolt composed entirely of girls from across the Land of Oz. Jinjur explains that the army intends to conquer the Emerald City, dethrone the Scarecrow, seize its gems and treasury, and rule the government themselves. Armed with knitting-needles, the girls overwhelm the Guardian of the Gate and force the Soldier with the Green Whiskers to flee because his gun is unloaded. The Army of Revolt captures the Emerald City without bloodshed.

The Scarecrow Plans an escape

Tip follows the Soldier with the Green Whiskers back to the Emerald City after a regiment of girls conquers it. The Scarecrow learns that the conquerors intend to use his straw and stuffing, so he plans an escape with Tip, Jack Pumpkinhead, and the Saw-Horse. The Soldier disguises himself by cutting off his green whiskers and helps tie the three passengers to the Saw-Horse. The Saw-Horse charges out of the Emerald City but launches them into a wide river.

The Journey to the Tin Woodman

Tip and his companions cross a river on the Saw-Horse, but the water soaks them and washes away Jack Pumpkinhead’s head while damaging the Scarecrow’s straw body. Tip retrieves Jack’s pumpkin head and dries and repairs the Scarecrow, after which they continue toward the Tin Woodman. Along the way, the Scarecrow recalls past adventures, and the group stops at night so the tired and hungry Tip can sleep.

A Nickel-Plated Emperor

Tip, the Scarecrow, Jack Pumpkinhead, and the Saw-Horse reach the City of the Winkies and visit the Tin Woodman, who is now its nickel-plated Emperor. The Tin Woodman welcomes them, helps restore the Scarecrow’s appearance, and agrees to accompany them to recapture the Emerald City from the girls of Oz. Tip repairs Jack Pumpkinhead, while the Tin Woodman prepares his kingdom for his absence. The group then begins the return journey to the Emerald City.

Mr. H. M. Woggle-Bug, T. E.

General Jinjur summons Mombi to help defend the Emerald City against the Scarecrow and Tin Woodman, and Mombi uses magic to hinder their Journey to the Emerald City. The travelers encounter an illusory field of whirling sunflowers with girls’ faces, then lose the Saw-Horse when he breaks a leg in a rabbit hole. They meet Mr. H. M. Woggle-Bug, T. E., a gigantic, highly educated insect who presents his card and begins to tell his history.

A Highly Magnified History

The Woggle-Bug recounts how he lived as a tiny insect near a schoolhouse, learned by listening to Professor Nowitall, and became highly magnified by the Professor's magnifying-glass. After escaping the schoolhouse, he obtained clothing from a tailor, met the Scarecrow, Tin Woodman, Saw-Horse, Pumpkinhead, and Tip, and joined their journey to the Emerald City. When the Saw-Horse broke a leg, the Woggle-Bug suggested using one of the Pumpkinhead's wooden legs as a replacement. The Tin Woodman amputated and reshaped Pumpkinhead's left leg, allowing the group to continue toward the Emerald City.

Old Mombi indulges in Witchcraft

Tip and his companions continue their journey to the Emerald City, with the Saw-Horse receiving a shorter replacement leg and the Woggle-Bug making unpopular puns. The Scarecrow recruits twelve field mice to hide in his straw and help him regain his throne, while the Queen of the Field Mice guides the travelers. Old Mombi's magical obstacles appear as deceptive illusions, including a river, a granite wall, whirling roads, and a sheet of flame. The travelers pass safely through the illusions and reach the gates of the Emerald City.

The Prisoners of the Queen

The Scarecrow, Tin Woodman, Tip, Woggle-Bug, Jack Pumpkinhead, and Saw-Horse enter the Emerald City and reach the royal palace, where General Jinjur sits on the throne. General Jinjur reveals that the Army of Revolt has surrounded them and orders that most of them be destroyed. The Scarecrow releases mice from his clothing, frightening the Army of Revolt and causing General Jinjur and the rebels to flee. The travelers then secure the royal palace and gather in the throne room to plan their next action.

The Scarecrow Takes Time to Think

The Scarecrow realizes that Jinjur remains Queen and that he and his friends are prisoners in her palace, which is surrounded by girl soldiers. Tip reveals that his nearly exhausted Powder of Life can bring things to life, prompting the Scarecrow to propose an escape through the air. Nick Chopper agrees to build a winged machine on the palace roof, which Tip can animate with the powder. The friends begin searching the palace for materials after Nick Chopper gives Jack Pumpkinhead a new wooden leg.

The Astonishing Flight of the Gump

The adventurers assemble materials on the roof of the palace to build a flying machine that can carry them to safety. The Tin Woodman joins two sofas, attaches a Gump’s head, four palm leaves, and Jack Pumpkinhead’s broom, and Tip uses magic powder to bring the Thing to life. The Thing’s wings create a dangerous wind, but its living Gump head obeys Tip’s command and guides it back to the roof.

In the Jackdaw’s Nest

The Gump carries Tip and the band of adventurers southward, but its first flight goes astray and ends in a crash in a Jackdaw’s Nest. During the journey, Tip and the others discover three of Dr. Nikidik’s Celebrated Wishing Pills hidden in the false bottom of the pepper-box, though they believe the instructions make them unusable. After landing outside the Land of Oz, the travelers find themselves trapped in the nest as the Jackdaws return. The Scarecrow hides Tip, Woggle-Bug, and Jack Pumpkinhead from the approaching birds by having the Tin Woodman disassemble him and spread his straw over them.

Dr. Nikidik’s Famous Wishing Pills

The Jackdaws attack the party in their nest, but the Tin Woodman’s axe, the Saw-Horse’s kicks, and the Gump’s flapping wings drive them away. The Jackdaws destroy the Scarecrow’s straw stuffing, so the others replace it with stolen money from the nest. Tip’s first wishing-pill experiment causes apparent pain and restores the pills after he wishes he had never swallowed one. The Woggle-Bug then uses a pill to wish the Gump’s broken wings mended, and the wish is immediately granted.

The Scarecrow Appeals to Glinda the Good

The Scarecrow and his companions escape the Jackdaws’ nest in the Gump and eventually reach Glinda the Good’s palace. The Scarecrow asks Glinda to help remove Queen Jinjur from the Emerald City, but Glinda explains that Ozma, the daughter of the former King Pastoria, is the rightful heir. Glinda searches her records and concludes that Mombi may have helped the Wizard of Oz hide Ozma. She prepares to march with her army to the Emerald City and capture Mombi.

The Tin-Woodman Plucks a Rose

Glinda the Good surrounds the Emerald City with her army and demands that Queen Jinjur surrender Mombi. Mombi deceives Glinda by transforming Jellia Jamb to resemble her and then transforming herself into a red rose in the palace garden. Glinda discovers the first deception and permits Jellia to remain under her protection, but her search for Mombi fails until the Tin Woodman unknowingly plucks the rose containing Mombi and carries it back to the camp.

The Transformation of Old Mombi

Mombi hides from Glinda by transforming herself into a rose worn in the Tin Woodman’s button-hole. Glinda discovers the rose is Mombi, but Mombi escapes by successively becoming a Shadow, a Black Ant, and a Griffin. Glinda pursues the Griffin on the Saw-Horse, eventually uses a golden thread to destroy the transformation, and reveals Mombi exhausted and captured in the desert.

Princess Ozma of Oz

Glinda captures Mombi and forces her to reveal that the Wonderful Wizard of Oz brought the baby Princess Ozma to her and paid her to conceal the child. Mombi admits that she enchanted Ozma into becoming Tip, who is therefore the rightful ruler of the Emerald City. Mombi reverses the enchantment, transforming Tip into the beautiful Princess Ozma, while Ozma worries that her friends will care for her less as a girl.

The Riches of Content

Queen Jinjur resists Glinda the Good and Princess Ozma after learning that Ozma has been discovered, but Glinda's army captures her by flying the Gump over the walls of the Emerald City. The Army of Revolt surrenders, Jinjur is released on promise of good behavior, and Princess Ozma takes the throne and restores order. Ozma later rewards the Gump by taking it apart, while the Tin Woodman and Scarecrow return to the Winkies together. The Scarecrow’s brains, the Tin Woodman’s good heart, and the riches of content are declared more valuable than money.
