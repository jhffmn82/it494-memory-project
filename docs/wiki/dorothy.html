Dorothy in The Wonderful Wizard of Oz

The Wonderful Wizard of Oz, 1900-04

Dorothy is an orphaned little girl from the Kansas prairies who lives with Aunt Em and Uncle Henry and loves Toto, her constant companion. Her deepest concern throughout is returning to Kansas: although it is gray and dreary, she believes there is no place like home and longs to live again with her family. A cyclone carries her house, with Dorothy and Toto inside, to a beautiful country, where the house kills the Wicked Witch of the East. The Witch of the North gives Dorothy the Silver Shoes, marks her with a protective kiss, and directs her to the Emerald City to ask Oz for help. Dorothy follows the yellow brick road with Toto and gains companions by freeing the Scarecrow, oiling the Tin Woodman, and defending Toto from the Cowardly Lion before welcoming him into the party. She shares food, seeks shelter and water, faces ditches, a river, a poppy field, and other dangers, and repeatedly shows care for her friends and for Toto. In the Emerald City, Oz promises to help her only if she kills the Wicked Witch of the West. Though she does not want to kill anyone, she travels west, is made a kitchen servant, secretly feeds the imprisoned Lion, and melts the Witch with water after the Witch takes one of her shoes. Dorothy frees the Lion and the Winkies, rescues her companions, gains the Golden Cap, and uses it to command the Winged Monkeys. Back in the Emerald City, Dorothy learns Oz is only a common man. His balloon attempt fails when she delays to find Toto, leaving her behind. Still determined, she travels south with her friends to Glinda, crossing difficult lands and using the Golden Cap again. At Glinda's castle, Dorothy gives up the cap, says goodbye to her companions, and uses the Silver Shoes to return with Toto to Aunt Em in Kansas, glad to be home again.

Chapter I

Dorothy is an orphan who lives with Uncle Henry and Aunt Em on the Kansas prairies. She tries to rescue Toto when the cyclone begins, remains inside the house as it is lifted into the air, and eventually falls asleep after deciding to wait calmly for what will happen.

Chapter II

Dorothy awakens after the cyclone lands her house in a beautiful country and meets the Witch of the North and three Munchkins. She learns that her house has killed the Wicked Witch of the East, receives the Witch's silver shoes, and is told to travel to the City of Emeralds to ask Oz for help returning home.

Chapter III

Dorothy prepares for her journey by gathering food, changing into a clean gingham dress, and putting on the silver shoes that belonged to the Wicked Witch of the East. She travels along the yellow brick road, stays overnight with Boq, and frees the Scarecrow from his pole. She invites the Scarecrow to accompany her to the Emerald City and promises to ask Oz to help him.

Chapter IV

Dorothy travels with the Scarecrow and Toto toward the Emerald City. She explains that, despite Kansas being gray and dreary, people of flesh and blood would rather live there than in any other country because there is no place like home.

Chapter V

Dorothy searches for water, discovers the rusted Tin Woodman, and oils his joints so he can move again. She agrees to let him join the party traveling to the Emerald City and carries his oil-can in her basket. She wants the Great Oz to send her back to Kansas and worries that the party's bread is nearly gone.

Chapter VI

Dorothy travels through the forest with Toto, the Tin Woodman, and the Scarecrow. She protects Toto by slapping the Lion and later agrees to let the Lion join them because he may help keep away other wild beasts. She plans to ask Oz to send her and Toto back to Kansas.

Chapter VII

Dorothy camps with the others, eats their last bread, and later gathers nuts and fruit for food. She crosses the first ditch on the Cowardly Lion's back, crosses the tree bridge during the Kalidah attack, and looks forward to reaching the Emerald City and returning home.

Chapter VIII

Dorothy crosses the river with her companions on the raft and later helps search for a way back to the road of yellow brick. In the poppy field, she becomes drowsy, falls asleep, and is carried by the Scarecrow and Tin Woodman to a safe grassy spot.

Chapter IX

Dorothy wakes to find thousands of field mice around her and learns how the Queen and the mice helped rescue the Cowardly Lion. She thanks the mice warmly and waits beside the Lion after he is brought out of the poppy bed.

Chapter X

Dorothy helps lead the travelers back to the road of yellow brick and onward toward the Emerald City. She asks a farming family for shelter, seeks the Great Oz's help in returning to Kansas, and enters the Emerald City wearing locked green spectacles.

Chapter XI

Dorothy enters the City of Oz with Toto and her companions and is given a comfortable room in the Palace of Oz. She asks Oz to send her back to Kansas, but he requires her to kill the Wicked Witch of the West first. Though she does not want to kill anyone, she agrees to undertake the task with her friends.

Chapter XII

Dorothy travels west with her companions and is carried unharmed to the Wicked Witch’s castle by the Winged Monkeys because of the mark of the Good Witch’s kiss. She is forced to work in the kitchen and secretly feeds the imprisoned Lion. After the Witch tricks her into losing one Silver Shoe, Dorothy throws water on her and remains free.

Chapter XIII

Dorothy unlocks the Cowardly Lion's prison and frees him, then tells the Winkies that they are no longer slaves. She leads the rescue of the Tin Woodman and the Scarecrow, reunites with both friends, and decides to return to Oz to get back to Kansas. Before leaving, she finds the Golden Cap, wears it, and starts for the Emerald City with her companions.

Chapter XIV

Dorothy becomes discouraged after traveling for days without finding the Emerald City, then calls the field mice for help. Following the Queen of the Mice's advice, she reads the charm inside the Golden Cap and summons the Winged Monkeys, who carry her safely to the Emerald City.

Chapter XV

Dorothy returns to the Emerald City with the other travelers after melting the Wicked Witch of the West. She confronts Oz about his promise to send her back to Kansas and learns that he is only a common man. She agrees to keep his secret while hoping he can still find a way to return her home.

Chapter XVI

Dorothy tells the Scarecrow that she has always liked him as he was. She looks at him curiously after Oz gives him his brains and remains the person Oz is unsure how to return to Kansas.

Chapter XVII

Dorothy helps Oz sew strips of silk together to make a balloon. She wants to return to Kansas and tries to board the balloon, but she is delayed while searching for Toto and is left behind when it rises.

Chapter XVIII

Dorothy weeps because she cannot return to Kansas and because Oz is gone. She asks the Winged Monkeys to carry her to Kansas, but they refuse because they cannot cross the desert. She agrees to travel with her companions to Glinda in the Land of the South and wants to start as soon as possible.

Chapter XIX

Dorothy says farewell to the Guardian of the Gate and expresses gratitude for how kindly she was treated in the Emerald City. She continues with her companions toward the Land of the South in hopes of returning to Kansas.

Chapter XX

Dorothy sleeps while the Tin Woodman builds a ladder, then climbs over the first wall with the others. In the Dainty China Country, she apologizes after a china cow breaks its leg and asks a china princess to come back to Kansas with her. She crosses the country carefully and leaves over the second wall.

Chapter XXI

Dorothy travels with the Lion, the Scarecrow, the Woodman, and Toto through bogs, marshes, and a forest. She is briefly afraid when they discover the gathering of wild animals.

Chapter XXII

Dorothy sees the Hammer-Heads on the hillside and helps the Scarecrow after he is knocked down. She uses the Golden Cap to summon the Winged Monkeys, who carry her and her companions over the hill, and she later asks for food and directions to Glinda’s castle.

Chapter XXIII

Dorothy tells Glinda the Good Witch about her arrival in Oz and her adventures. She gives Glinda the Golden Cap, says goodbye to her companions, and uses the Silver Shoes to return to Aunt Em in Kansas.

Chapter XXIV

Dorothy returns from the Land of Oz with Toto and tells Aunt Em that she is glad to be home again.

Dorothy in Ozma of Oz

Ozma of Oz, 1907

Dorothy Gale is a little Kansas girl, Uncle Henry's niece, an experienced traveller whose earlier adventures have taken her to the Land of Oz. While traveling with Uncle Henry toward Australia, she is carried from a storm-tossed ship in a chicken-coop, survives adrift at sea, and reaches an unknown land. There she befriends and names Billina, finds food and a dinner-pail, escapes the Wheelers, discovers and winds Tiktok, and travels with Billina and Tiktok toward Ev. After refusing Princess Langwidere's demand for her head, Dorothy is imprisoned until Ozma and her companions arrive to free her. Dorothy reunites joyfully with the Scarecrow, Tin Woodman, Cowardly Lion, and others, then joins Ozma's expedition to rescue the Queen of Ev and her children from the Nome King. She argues that their imprisonment is wrong, warns Ozma against the King's dangerous guessing condition, and remains brave despite fear when her friends are transformed into ornaments. Entering the enchanted palace, she makes guesses and correctly identifies Prince Evring as a purple kitten, rescuing him. Protected by the Lion and Hungry Tiger, she later takes the Nome King's magic belt at Billina's urging. Dorothy uses the belt to open a passage, turn pursuing warriors into eggs, and stop the giant of iron, while continuing to seek the restoration of her friends. She returns with Ozma's party to the Land of Oz, where she is welcomed, given a coronet, and made a Princess of Oz. Yet her concern for Uncle Henry, seen through Ozma's magic picture in Australia, matters more than remaining in Oz. She gives the magic belt to Ozma, says farewell to her friends, and is transported to Australia to return to him.

front matter .. Author's Note

Dorothy Gale is the central child character whose further adventures the children asked Baum to write about. Baum says this book includes more about Dorothy and has her meet Ozma and spend time with her.

1. The Girl in the Chicken Coop

Dorothy Gale travels with Uncle Henry toward Australia and is not easily frightened because of her previous adventures. Worried that Uncle Henry has gone onto the dangerous deck, she searches for him, clings to a chicken-coop when the wind lifts it away, and calmly survives on it after it falls into the ocean. Once the storm ends, she waits patiently and falls asleep.

2. The Yellow Hen

Dorothy Gale awakens in the floating hen-coop after the storm and talks with the yellow hen, whom she renames Billina. She reaches the unknown land, dries her shoes and stockings, helps uncover a golden key, and decides to search for breakfast.

3. Letters in the Sand

Dorothy reads the warning in the sand, searches the forest for food, and finds trees bearing lunch-boxes and dinner-pails. She eats the food from a lunch-box and picks a dinner-pail. After the Wheelers chase her, she escapes with Billina onto a rocky hill, where she remains trapped but safe from them.

4. Tiktok the Machine Man

Dorothy follows a hidden path to the top of the mound with Billina and uses the golden key to open a rocky door. She winds the copper machine's mechanisms, learns his history, and accepts Tiktok as her obedient servant.

5. Dorothy Opens the Dinner Pail

Dorothy searches for Tiktok's lost key, rewinds his voice and action machinery, and listens to his explanation of the Wheelers. She eats from her dinner pail, discusses the Land of Oz with Billina and Tiktok, and follows Tiktok toward Evna.

6. The Heads of Langwidere

Dorothy Gale helps Tiktok keep the Wheelers' secret and travels with Tiktok and Billina to the royal palace. She refuses Princess Langwidere's demand to exchange her head for head No. 26, after which Langwidere orders her imprisoned in the North Tower.

7. Ozma of Oz to the Rescue

Dorothy Gale is held prisoner in a tower because she refuses to exchange her head for one from Princess Langwidere's collection. She sees Ozma of Oz and her friends arrive, calls to them for help, and is released by Langwidere.

8. The Hungry Tiger

Dorothy joyfully greets the Scarecrow, Tin Woodman, Cowardly Lion, and Hungry Tiger. She frees Billina from the chicken house, introduces her to the Lion and Tiger, and joins Ozma, Princess Langwidere, Tiktok, and the Army of Oz for the conference about liberating the royal family.

9. The Royal Family of Ev

Dorothy argues that imprisoning a queen mother and her ten children is wrong and agrees to join Ozma's expedition. She spends time with her friends, meets the Sawhorse, and rides beside Ozma in the golden chariot while holding Billina.

10. The Giant with the Hammer

Dorothy explains that Billina must stop to lay her daily egg and later rides the Hungry Tiger beneath the iron mallet. She helps Ozma hold on to the Lion's mane and is present while the army and private are rescued.

11. The Nome King

Dorothy encourages Ozma to plead with the Nome King and personally asks him to appear. She questions the King's claim that King of Ev's long life was fulfilled and accompanies Ozma into the palace.

12. The Eleven Guesses

Dorothy warns Ozma not to accept the challenge because a wrong guess would enslave her. After Ozma fails, Dorothy is prepared to attempt the rescue, but the Tin Woodman claims the privilege of going next.

13. The Nome King Laughs

Dorothy eats cakes and drinks coffee made from richly flavored clay while most of the party is transformed into ornaments. She becomes anxious when Billina is missing, but cannot open the locked door to the throne room and eventually falls asleep, expecting that she may become an ornament the next day.

14. Dorothy Tries to be Brave

Dorothy bravely enters the Nome King's palace to wind up Tiktok and make guesses about the enchanted ornaments. She fails to identify several objects but correctly guesses that a purple kitten is Prince Evring, then returns to the throne room with him.

15. Billina Frightens the Nome King

Dorothy correctly guesses one enchantment and returns safely from the enchanted palace with Prince Evring. She warns Billina that the guesses are difficult and invites her to return to the Land of Ev instead.

17. The Scarecrow Wins the Fight

Dorothy waits with Prince Evring for Billina's mission to succeed and rejoices as the bell announces each restoration. She stands protected by the Lion, Hungry Tiger, and Sawhorse, and is later instructed by Billina to take the Nome King's belt.

18. The Fate of the Tin Woodman

Dorothy takes the Nome King's jeweled magic belt and fastens it around her waist. She uses it to open the passage, turn pursuing warriors into eggs, and stop the giant of iron, but she cannot find the transformed Tin Woodman.

19. The King of Ev

Dorothy rides with Evring on the Sawhorse and chats pleasantly with him. She is overjoyed when Billina restores the Tin Woodman and hugs her old friend.

20. The Emerald City

Dorothy decides to return with Ozma to the Land of Oz because she has no better chance of getting home from Ev and wants to revisit Oz. She is welcomed by the people of Oz, receives a pretty coronet, and is made a Princess of Oz.

21. Dorothy's Magic Belt

Dorothy uses Ozma's magic picture to look at the Kansas farm and Uncle Henry in Australia. Worried by Uncle Henry's poor condition, she decides to return to him, gives the magic belt to Ozma, and says farewell to her friends in the Land of Oz before being transported to Australia.
