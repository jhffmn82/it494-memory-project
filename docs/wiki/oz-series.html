They cover Dorothy’s journeys in the Land of Oz, including her arrival with Toto, and further adventures involving Tip, Jack Pumpkinhead, the Saw-Horse, Billina, and Tiktok. The Wonderful Wizard of Oz

1900-04

This document is a modernized fairy tale introduced by L. Frank Baum as a story meant to preserve wonder and joy without the heartaches and nightmares of older tales. It follows Dorothy, a Kansas girl swept away with her dog Toto when a cyclone carries her house to the land of the Munchkins. Her house kills the Wicked Witch of the East, freeing the Munchkins, and the Witch of the North gives Dorothy enchanted Silver Shoes and sends her along the yellow brick road to ask the Great Oz to help her return home. On the road Dorothy gains three companions: the Scarecrow, who wants brains; the Tin Woodman, who wants a heart after the Wicked Witch of the East turned him to tin; and the Cowardly Lion, who wants courage. Together they survive forests, gulfs, a river, deadly poppies, and other dangers, aided at times by field mice. At the Emerald City, Oz appears differently to each traveler and demands that they kill the Wicked Witch of the West before he will grant their wishes. In the Winkies’ country, the Witch attacks the group with wolves, crows, bees, and Winged Monkeys. Dorothy is captured, but she melts the Witch by throwing water on her, frees the Winkies, and reunites her companions. Back in the Emerald City, the travelers discover Oz is an ordinary man and a humbug who created frightening appearances with tricks. Still, he gives the Scarecrow, Tin Woodman, and Lion objects that persuade them they have received brains, a heart, and courage. His attempt to take Dorothy home by hot-air balloon fails when Toto delays her and Oz floats away alone. Dorothy and her friends then travel south to Glinda, passing the Dainty China Country, helping the Lion become King of the Forest, and crossing the Hammer-Heads’ hill with the Winged Monkeys. Glinda reveals that Dorothy’s Silver Shoes could have carried her home all along. After arranging homes for her companions, Glinda tells Dorothy to use the shoes. Dorothy returns to Kansas with Toto, loses the shoes in the desert, and is joyfully welcomed by Aunt Em.

The Marvelous Land of Oz

1904-06

The Marvelous Land of Oz is a fantasy sequel introduced by L. Frank Baum as a response to children who wanted more of the Scarecrow and Tin Woodman; it is dedicated to David C. Montgomery and Frank A. Stone. It begins with Tip, a youth mistreated by the sorceress Mombi in the Country of the Gillikins. Tip creates Jack Pumpkinhead to frighten her, but Mombi brings Jack to life with the Powder of Life and plans to turn Tip into marble. Tip escapes with Jack and the powder, animates a wooden Saw-Horse, and heads for the Emerald City. Jack reaches the Scarecrow, ruler of the Emerald City, while Tip encounters General Jinjur’s all-girl Army of Revolt. Jinjur’s soldiers seize the city without bloodshed, forcing Tip, the Scarecrow, Jack, and the Saw-Horse to flee. They seek aid from the Tin Woodman, now nickel-plated Emperor of the Winkies, and are joined by the highly educated Woggle-Bug. Mombi, aiding Jinjur, blocks their return with illusions, but they reach the palace. The Scarecrow’s hidden field mice frighten the rebels away, though the friends remain trapped by Jinjur’s surrounding army. Using Tip’s remaining Powder of Life, the party animates a winged machine with a Gump’s head and escapes. After crashing in a Jackdaw’s Nest, they survive attacks, repair the Gump with a wishing pill, and fly to Glinda the Good. Glinda says the true ruler is Ozma, daughter of King Pastoria, and suspects Mombi concealed her. Glinda’s army surrounds the Emerald City, captures Mombi despite her transformations, and compels her confession: the Wizard of Oz brought baby Ozma to her, and Mombi enchanted Ozma into Tip. Mombi restores Tip as Princess Ozma, the rightful ruler. Although Ozma fears her friends may no longer care for her, Glinda’s forces defeat Jinjur, whose army surrenders. Ozma takes the throne, restores order, and releases Jinjur on a promise of good behavior. The Gump is rewarded by being taken apart, while the Tin Woodman and Scarecrow return to the Winkies, where brains, heart, and contentment are valued above money.

Ozma of Oz

1907

This is L. Frank Baum’s Ozma of Oz, presented with publication material and an author’s note in which Baum says children’s letters prompted him to continue the adventures of Dorothy Gale, Ozma, the Cowardly Lion, and others. The story begins when a sea storm carries Dorothy away from Uncle Henry in a chicken coop. On the ocean she meets Billina, a talking yellow hen, and they drift to the Land of Ev. Pursued by Wheelers after taking a dinner-pail, they discover Tiktok, a copper machine man who must be wound to think, speak, and act. He tells them of Ev’s former King Evoldo, who sold his wife and ten children to the Nome King. At Ev’s palace, Princess Langwidere imprisons Dorothy because she wants Dorothy’s head for her collection. Ozma arrives from Oz with the Scarecrow, Nick Chopper, the Cowardly Lion, the Hungry Tiger, soldiers, and others, frees Dorothy, and organizes an expedition to rescue Ev’s lost royal family. After crossing dangerous country, including a gulf and an iron giant with a hammer, the party reaches the Nome King’s underground palace. The Nome King says he transformed the Queen of Ev and her children into ornaments and offers their freedom only if the visitors can identify them. Ozma, the Tin Woodman, the Army of Oz, the Scarecrow, and others fail and are transformed. Dorothy restores Prince Evring but cannot save the rest. Billina, however, discovers that eggs terrify Nomes, gains entry to the enchanted rooms, and successfully identifies and restores the Queen, her children, Ozma, the Scarecrow, Tiktok, and the people of Oz. The Scarecrow drives off the Nome King’s warriors with Billina’s eggs, and Dorothy takes the King’s magic belt. The party escapes, later discovers that the Tin Woodman is a tin whistle, and Billina restores him. Back in Ev, Prince Evring’s eldest brother is crowned King Evardo Fifteenth. Dorothy then travels with her friends to the Emerald City, where she is made a Princess of Oz. Finally, seeing through Ozma’s magic picture that Uncle Henry is ill in Australia, Dorothy gives Ozma the magic belt and returns to nurse him, with Ozma promising to bring her back when Dorothy signals.
