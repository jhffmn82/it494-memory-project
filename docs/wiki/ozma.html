Tip in The Marvelous Land of Oz

The Marvelous Land of Oz, 1904-06

Tip, whose full name is Tippetarius, begins as a strong, rugged yet small and delicate youth raised by Mombi, with no memory of his parents. He works for her, openly hates her, and fears her plan to turn him into a marble statue. While she is absent, he creates Jack Pumpkinhead by carving a pumpkin head and building a wooden body, first to scare Mombi and then as an experiment. He repairs Jack and uses Mombi’s Powder of Life to animate him. When threatened, Tip flees with Jack, food, and the stolen powder toward the Emerald City. On the journey, Tip experiments again, bringing the Saw-Horse to life and teaching it commands. Though poor, mortal, and often tired or hungry, he remains practical and protective: he repairs Jack, helps his companions, warns them of danger, and seeks to reunite with Jack and the Saw-Horse after being left behind. He becomes involved with General Jinjur’s revolt, joins the Scarecrow’s escape from the conquered Emerald City, and travels with the Scarecrow, Jack, the Saw-Horse, the Tin Woodman, and others. Tip uses the remaining Powder of Life to animate the Gump, helps direct its flight, and later refuses another wishing pill after undoing its effects with a wish. He reports Mombi’s threats and interference to Glinda and travels with Glinda’s party back to the Emerald City. At the end, Mombi’s enchantment is reversed: Tip is revealed to be Princess Ozma, a girl and the rightful ruler of the Emerald City. Ozma hopes that the friends made while living as Tip will still care for her.

Tip appears in the chapter list as the maker of Pumpkinhead and as someone who makes an experiment in magic.

Tip is a strong, rugged youth raised by Mombi, whom he openly dislikes. While Mombi is away, he carves a pumpkin, constructs a wooden human figure, dresses it in clothing, and names it Jack Pumpkinhead.

Tip repairs Jack Pumpkinhead, places him at the bend in the road, and hides to watch Mombi. After Jack comes to life, Tip laughs at him and is taken into the house, where Mombi prepares to transform him into a marble statue.

Tip decides to run away from Mombi to avoid becoming a marble statue. He takes food and the Powder of Life, rescues Jack Pumpkinhead, and travels south with him toward the Emerald City.

Tip worries that Jack’s wooden joints are too weak and advises him to remain standing after he collapses while sitting. He explains the nature of the saw-horse and decides to sprinkle it with the Powder of Life. Tip repeats Mombi’s words and gestures, causing the saw-horse to move.

Tip brings the Saw-Horse to life and tries to control it when it circles, bumps into Jack Pumpkinhead, and steps on Tip’s foot. He makes bark ears for the Saw-Horse, teaches it that “Whoa!” means stop, “Get-Up!” means walk forward, and “Trot!” means go fast, then prepares it to carry Jack.

Tip awakens at daybreak, prepares for the journey, and leads the Saw-Horse and Jack Pumpkinhead toward the Emerald City. After crossing the river, he tells the Saw-Horse to go faster, loses his grip on its tail, and is left behind. He rests, then walks along the yellow brick road, expecting to catch up with them at the city gates.

Tip meets General Jinjur while walking toward the Emerald City and accepts food from her lunch basket. He follows her to the Army of Revolt, carries packages for its members, and witnesses the capture of the Emerald City.

Tip follows the Soldier with the Green Whiskers to the conquered Emerald City and warns the Scarecrow that the palace is surrounded. He joins the escape, riding at the front of the Saw-Horse, and receives a small prick in his left arm from a knitting-needle before being plunged into the river.

Tip becomes soaked during the river crossing but helps guide the Saw-Horse to shore. He retrieves Jack Pumpkinhead’s head, dries and repairs the Scarecrow, and then continues traveling toward the Tin Woodman. At night, Tip says he is tired and hungry and sleeps on the grass with the Scarecrow as his pillow.

Tip eats berries for breakfast, travels with the Scarecrow, Jack Pumpkinhead, and the Saw-Horse to the City of the Winkies, and observes the Tin Woodman’s entirely metal construction. He tells the Tin Woodman that the Saw-Horse was brought to life with magic powder and later mends Jack Pumpkinhead’s wooden limbs.

Tip identifies the sunflower field as witchcraft and suspects that Mombi is creating an illusion. He leads the group forward with the Saw-Horse while keeping his eyes closed, and later joins the others in thinking of a way to repair the broken leg.

Tip tells the Pumpkinhead to keep quiet and points out that the group cannot leave him behind because he needs to ride the Saw-Horse. Tip also observes that insects do not normally wear clothes.

Tip objects to the Woggle-Bug's pun and says that anybody can make a pun. He is the only member of the party who becomes tired, so the others repeatedly stop to let him rest, and he eventually says that he is ready to continue.

Tip accompanies the Scarecrow and the others into the Emerald City. He warns that their unopposed progress is too easy and may be a trap.

Tip reveals that he possesses a nearly depleted pepper-box of Powder of Life, which Mombi obtained from a crooked sorcerer. He previously used the powder to bring Jack Pumpkinhead and the Saw-Horse to life, and he agrees to use the remaining dose on the proposed flying machine.

Tip and the Saw-Horse bring sofas to the roof, and Tip sprinkles the assembled Thing with magic powder. He brings it to life with a magic charm, clings to a chimney during its first flight, and commands it to return.

Tip tells the Gump that it was created to carry the travelers through the air and directs it to fly south without going too high. He discovers the false bottom of the pepper-box and reads the directions for Dr. Nikidik’s Celebrated Wishing Pills. After the crash, he helps identify the nest and follows the Scarecrow’s instructions to hide with Woggle-Bug and Jack Pumpkinhead.

Tip suggests stuffing the Scarecrow with money and takes the first wishing pill. After experiencing apparent pain, he wishes he had never swallowed it, causing the pill to be back in the box; he then refuses to take another.

Tip travels with the others in the Gump and identifies the South Country when he sees red houses, fences, and trees. He reports that Mombi is with Queen Jinjur in the Emerald City and that she has hindered their journey and threatened his friends.

Tip travels with Glinda and the others and walks beside the Sorceress after she enters the Emerald City. Glinda has developed a great liking for him.

Princess Ozma is revealed to be the child who was transformed into Tip and the rightful ruler of the Emerald City. After Mombi reverses the enchantment, she appears as a beautiful young girl and says she hopes her friends will still care for her.

Ozma in Ozma of Oz

Ozma of Oz, 1907

Ozma, known as Ozma of Oz, is a beautiful, dainty girl, royal princess, rightful heir to the throne, and supreme ruler of the Land of Oz, living in Emerald City. She rules Oz with authority but is chiefly defined here by her resolve to rescue others. After deposing General Jinjur, she frees Dorothy and her friends, then convenes a solemn conference when she learns that King Evoldo sold the Queen of Ev and her ten children to the Nome King. Determined that the prisoners must be liberated, she organizes and commands an expedition from Oz, traveling in her golden chariot, using Glinda the Good's magic carpet to cross a gulf, and leading the party to the Nome King's palace. Rather than fight his overwhelming army, she accepts his challenge to identify the transformed royal family. Her eleven guesses fail, and she is transformed into a small emerald grasshopper. Once restored, she continues the rescue effort, orders her army to fight, refuses to depart without the Tin Woodman, and leads the party back through danger. After the Nome King's defeat, she returns the Queen mother and royal children to Ev, then brings her companions safely back to Oz, where she is welcomed by her subjects and promotes Omby Amby. At the end, she uses her magic picture with Dorothy, accepts Dorothy's magic belt, and agrees to help Dorothy return to Uncle Henry and later come back to Oz. Her ivory wand, magic picture, chariot, army, and authority show her power, including power enough to destroy the Land of Ev, but her actions emphasize liberation, protection, and return.

Ozma is described as having become the Ruler of Oz. Children asked Baum what she did afterward, and he says the book brings Ozma and Dorothy together.

Ozma leads the party to the Nome King's domain and asks him to release the Queen of Ev and her ten children. She refuses to fight the Nome King's overwhelming army and agrees to attempt his challenge, risking transformation if she identifies none of the correct objects.

Ozma accepts the Nome King's offer and enters his royal palace to identify transformations of the royal family of Ev. After ten failed guesses, she uses her eleventh guess blindly and is transformed into a pretty grasshopper formed from a single emerald.

Ozma of Oz was transformed into a small green grasshopper, which Billina accidentally catches and then recognizes as a lifeless transformation. After being restored, Ozma helps search for the Scarecrow and Tiktok and decides that the group must return to the Nome King to find the Tin Woodman.

Ozma is restored to her natural form and orders her army to fight the Nome King's forces. Her generals avoid fighting because they claim to suffer from heart disease, so they order the private soldier to attack.

Ozma welcomes the rescued people and refuses to leave without finding the Tin Woodman. She leads the returning party and notices the Nome King's warriors pursuing them.

Ozma returns to Ev with Dorothy, the Queen mother, and the royal children after the defeat of the Nome King. She welcomes the restored Tin Woodman at the royal palace and is splendidly entertained by the Queen mother.

Ozma leads the party across the desert on her magic carpet and returns safely to the Land of Oz. She is welcomed by her subjects, receives a grand reception, and promotes Omby Amby to Captain General of all the armies and Commander of her Body Guard.

Ozma lets Dorothy use the magic picture and agrees to help her return to Uncle Henry. She accepts the magic belt and agrees to use it to bring Dorothy back to the Land of Oz when Dorothy gives the agreed signal.
