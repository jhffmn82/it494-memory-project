Tip in The Marvelous Land of Oz

The Marvelous Land of Oz, 1904-06

Tip, whose full name is Tippetarius, begins as a strong, rugged yet small and delicate youth raised by Mombi, with no memory of his parents. He works for her, openly hates her, and fears her plan to turn him into a marble statue. While she is absent, he creates Jack Pumpkinhead by carving a pumpkin head and building a wooden body, first to scare Mombi and then as an experiment. He repairs Jack and uses Mombi’s Powder of Life to animate him. When threatened, Tip flees with Jack, food, and the stolen powder toward the Emerald City. On the journey, Tip experiments again, bringing the Saw-Horse to life and teaching it commands. Though poor, mortal, and often tired or hungry, he remains practical and protective: he repairs Jack, helps his companions, warns them of danger, and seeks to reunite with Jack and the Saw-Horse after being left behind. He becomes involved with General Jinjur’s revolt, joins the Scarecrow’s escape from the conquered Emerald City, and travels with the Scarecrow, Jack, the Saw-Horse, the Tin Woodman, and others. Tip uses the remaining Powder of Life to animate the Gump, helps direct its flight, and later refuses another wishing pill after undoing its effects with a wish. He reports Mombi’s threats and interference to Glinda and travels with Glinda’s party back to the Emerald City. At the end, Mombi’s enchantment is reversed: Tip is revealed to be Princess Ozma, a girl and the rightful ruler of the Emerald City. Ozma hopes that the friends made while living as Tip will still care for her.

Ozma in The Marvelous Land of Oz

The Marvelous Land of Oz, 1904-06

Ozma is a princess of Oz, daughter of Pastoria and rightful heir to the Emerald City. Hidden by the Wizard of Oz through a magical trick, she is lost until discovered as Tip, the long-lost Princess Ozma. She helps end Jinjur’s revolt, becomes Queen, restores stolen gems, disbands the Army of Revolt, releases Jinjur on good behavior, dismantles the Gump, appoints Scarecrow Royal Treasurer, and rules with wisdom and justice.

Ozma in Ozma of Oz

Ozma of Oz, 1907

Ozma, known as Ozma of Oz, is a beautiful, dainty girl, royal princess, rightful heir to the throne, and supreme ruler of the Land of Oz, living in Emerald City. She rules Oz with authority but is chiefly defined here by her resolve to rescue others. After deposing General Jinjur, she frees Dorothy and her friends, then convenes a solemn conference when she learns that King Evoldo sold the Queen of Ev and her ten children to the Nome King. Determined that the prisoners must be liberated, she organizes and commands an expedition from Oz, traveling in her golden chariot, using Glinda the Good's magic carpet to cross a gulf, and leading the party to the Nome King's palace. Rather than fight his overwhelming army, she accepts his challenge to identify the transformed royal family. Her eleven guesses fail, and she is transformed into a small emerald grasshopper. Once restored, she continues the rescue effort, orders her army to fight, refuses to depart without the Tin Woodman, and leads the party back through danger. After the Nome King's defeat, she returns the Queen mother and royal children to Ev, then brings her companions safely back to Oz, where she is welcomed by her subjects and promotes Omby Amby. At the end, she uses her magic picture with Dorothy, accepts Dorothy's magic belt, and agrees to help Dorothy return to Uncle Henry and later come back to Oz. Her ivory wand, magic picture, chariot, army, and authority show her power, including power enough to destroy the Land of Ev, but her actions emphasize liberation, protection, and return.

Ozma of Oz was transformed into a small green grasshopper, which Billina accidentally catches and then recognizes as a lifeless transformation. After being restored, Ozma helps search for the Scarecrow and Tiktok and decides that the group must return to the Nome King to find the Tin Woodman.

Ozma leads the party across the desert on her magic carpet and returns safely to the Land of Oz. She is welcomed by her subjects, receives a grand reception, and promotes Omby Amby to Captain General of all the armies and Commander of her Body Guard.

21. Dorothy's Magic Belt

Ozma lets Dorothy use the magic picture and agrees to help her return to Uncle Henry. She accepts the magic belt and agrees to use it to bring Dorothy back to the Land of Oz when Dorothy gives the agreed signal.
