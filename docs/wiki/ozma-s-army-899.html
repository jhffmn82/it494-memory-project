In Ozma of Oz: Ozma's army is a group of twenty-seven warriors, called the finest soldiers of Oz: eight Generals, six Colonels, seven Majors, five Captains, and one private, making twenty-six officers under the private soldier. It surrounds an important group in the palace drawing-room, then is assembled for Ozma's expedition and leaves prepared to fight the Nome King if needed. In the valley it accompanies Ozma's procession, tumbles when it stops for Billina, and crosses beneath a hammer with help for its officers and private. Scarecrow leads it on the return march.
