This is L. Frank Baum’s Ozma of Oz, presented with publication material and an author’s note in which Baum says children’s letters prompted him to continue the adventures of Dorothy Gale, Ozma, the Cowardly Lion, and others. The story begins when a sea storm carries Dorothy away from Uncle Henry in a chicken coop. On the ocean she meets Billina, a talking yellow hen, and they drift to the Land of Ev. Pursued by Wheelers after taking a dinner-pail, they discover Tiktok, a copper machine man who must be wound to think, speak, and act. He tells them of Ev’s former King Evoldo, who sold his wife and ten children to the Nome King. At Ev’s palace, Princess Langwidere imprisons Dorothy because she wants Dorothy’s head for her collection. Ozma arrives from Oz with the Scarecrow, Nick Chopper, the Cowardly Lion, the Hungry Tiger, soldiers, and others, frees Dorothy, and organizes an expedition to rescue Ev’s lost royal family. After crossing dangerous country, including a gulf and an iron giant with a hammer, the party reaches the Nome King’s underground palace. The Nome King says he transformed the Queen of Ev and her children into ornaments and offers their freedom only if the visitors can identify them. Ozma, the Tin Woodman, the Army of Oz, the Scarecrow, and others fail and are transformed. Dorothy restores Prince Evring but cannot save the rest. Billina, however, discovers that eggs terrify Nomes, gains entry to the enchanted rooms, and successfully identifies and restores the Queen, her children, Ozma, the Scarecrow, Tiktok, and the people of Oz. The Scarecrow drives off the Nome King’s warriors with Billina’s eggs, and Dorothy takes the King’s magic belt. The party escapes, later discovers that the Tin Woodman is a tin whistle, and Billina restores him. Back in Ev, Prince Evring’s eldest brother is crowned King Evardo Fifteenth. Dorothy then travels with her friends to the Emerald City, where she is made a Princess of Oz. Finally, seeing through Ozma’s magic picture that Uncle Henry is ill in Australia, Dorothy gives Ozma the magic belt and returns to nurse him, with Ozma promising to bring her back when Dorothy signals.

front matter .. Author's Note

The unit presents the Project Gutenberg edition of L. Frank Baum's Ozma of Oz, including its title, publication information, contents, and author’s note. Baum explains that children’s letters inspired him to write more about Dorothy Gale, the Cowardly Lion, Ozma, and other characters. He says the book includes the Scarecrow, the Tin Woodman, Billina, Tiktok, the Hungry Tiger, and other new and unusual people. Baum hopes his young readers will find the story “real Ozzy” and will write to tell him how they like it.

1. The Girl in the Chicken Coop

A severe storm at sea separates Dorothy Gale from her Uncle Henry when the wind lifts the chicken-coop she is holding onto and carries it into the ocean. Dorothy remains calm, climbs onto the coop’s wooden bottom, and watches the ship disappear into the distance. After the wind stops and the waves calm, she waits patiently and falls asleep in the damp coop beneath the moon and stars.

2. The Yellow Hen

Dorothy Gale awakens in a floating hen-coop on the ocean and discovers that a yellow hen can speak. The hen, whom Dorothy renames Billina, helps her realize that they are drifting toward an unknown land. They reach the shore, where Billina searches for food and finds a large golden key buried in the sand. Dorothy keeps the key and sets out to look for breakfast.

3. Letters in the Sand

Dorothy and Billina find the warning “BEWARE THE WHEELERS!” written in the sand near a forest. Dorothy discovers trees bearing lunch-boxes and dinner-pails, eats a lunch-box meal, and takes a dinner-pail. A group of Wheelers chases them after accusing Dorothy of stealing, forcing them to take refuge on a rocky hill. The Wheelers cannot cross the rocks, but they surround the hill and threaten Dorothy under a law requiring anyone who picks a dinner-pail without permission to die.

4. Tiktok the Machine Man

Dorothy and Billina discover a hidden rocky chamber using a golden key and find a copper mechanical man inside. Dorothy reads his instructions, winds his thinking, speaking, and action mechanisms, and learns that he was made by Smith & Tinker and purchased by Evoldo, the cruel King of Ev. The machine, named Tiktok, explains that Evoldo sold his wife and ten children to the Nome King, then locked Tiktok away and drowned while trying to recover them. Tiktok becomes Dorothy's obedient servant, provided that she keeps him wound.

5. Dorothy Opens the Dinner Pail

Dorothy rewinds Tiktok after he runs down while warning her about the Wheelers, who threaten to kill them. She eats from her dinner pail and learns that it belongs to the royal family of Ev, whose kingdom is also inhabited by people besides the Wheelers. Tiktok explains that the Land of Oz lies beyond a broad desert and that Ozma of Oz now rules it after deposing General Jinjur. After thinking of a way out, Tiktok leads Dorothy and Billina toward the town of Evna to escape the rocks and avoid the Wheelers.

6. The Heads of Langwidere

Dorothy Gale, Tiktok, and Billina leave the Wheelers behind and travel to the royal palace in the Land of Ev. Tiktok defeats and questions a Wheeler leader, learning that Princess Langwidere is the nearest thing to a ruler and changes among thirty different heads. Langwidere demands Dorothy's head for her collection, but Dorothy refuses and is imprisoned in the North Tower. Tiktok runs down while defending Dorothy, and Billina is sent to the chicken-house.

7. Ozma of Oz to the Rescue

Dorothy Gale, imprisoned in a tower by Princess Langwidere, sees Ozma of Oz arrive from the Land of Oz with the Scarecrow, Nick Chopper, the Cowardly Lion, and twenty-seven soldiers. Ozma confronts Princess Langwidere and agrees to help free the royal family of Ev, but first orders Dorothy's release. Dorothy is freed, while the Scarecrow and Nick Chopper speak with Tiktok about brains, hearts, and machinery.

8. The Hungry Tiger

Dorothy reunites with the Scarecrow, Tin Woodman, Tiktok, Cowardly Lion, and Hungry Tiger, then frees Billina from the palace chicken house. The Hungry Tiger explains that he is constantly hungry but refuses to eat living creatures because his conscience tells him it is wrong. Billina has fought a speckled rooster and is injured but defiant. In the palace, Ozma announces a solemn conference to decide how to liberate the royal family of the Land of Ev from their long imprisonment.

9. The Royal Family of Ev

Ozma of Oz leads a planned expedition from Ev to the Nome King's underground palace to liberate the former Queen of Ev and her ten children. Tiktok explains that King Evoldo sold his family to Roquat of the Rocks and later drowned himself, changing Ozma's understanding of the situation but not her resolve to free the prisoners. Dorothy, the Scarecrow, the Tin Woodman, Tiktok, Billina, the Cowardly Lion, the Hungry Tiger, the Sawhorse, and the Army of Oz join the undertaking, while Princess Langwidere stays behind. The party leaves the palace at daybreak and begins traveling toward the Nome King's domain.

10. The Giant with the Hammer

Ozma's procession travels through a mountain valley and uses a magic carpet to cross a gulf before encountering an iron giant blocking the narrow path with its iron mallet. The Scarecrow devises a plan to cross beneath the raised hammer, and Dorothy, Ozma, the Cowardly Lion, the Hungry Tiger, Tiktok, the Tin Woodman, and others make it safely across. The Lion and Tiger repeatedly rescue the officers, while the Sawhorse is struck by the hammer and the Scarecrow is injured while rescuing the private. After the private is saved and the Sawhorse is repaired, the party continues onward, leaving the giant pounding behind them.

11. The Nome King

Ozma, Dorothy, and their companions reach the Nome King's underground palace seeking to rescue the Queen of Ev and her ten children. The Nome King says that King of Ev sold them to him in exchange for a long life, and that he transformed them into ornaments and bric-a-brac rather than making them labor as slaves. After displaying his vast army, he offers Ozma a chance to free them by correctly identifying their transformations among eleven objects. If Ozma fails to identify any of them, she will herself be transformed into an ornament or article of bric-a-brac.

12. The Eleven Guesses

Ozma accepts the Nome King's challenge to make eleven guesses in his palace, hoping to free members of the royal family of Ev. She makes ten guesses without success and uses her final guess blindly, but she is transformed into a grasshopper made from a single emerald. The Nome King then invites another attempt, offering twelve guesses because twelve people are now transformed into ornaments. The Tin Woodman volunteers to go next, despite Dorothy's willingness to try.

13. The Nome King Laughs

The Nome King laughs at the Tin Woodman's transformation into an ornament and sends the adventurers into his palace to make guesses, turning all twenty-six officers and the private into ornaments when they fail. Dorothy, the Scarecrow, Tiktok, the private, and the animals remain outside while the officers disappear into the palace, and the Nome King eventually sends everyone to sleeping rooms. Billina hides beneath the Nome King's throne and is accidentally left behind when the others retire. Dorothy worries about Billina, but she eventually falls asleep despite fearing that she may become an ornament the next day.

14. Dorothy Tries to be Brave

The Nome King reveals to his Chief Steward that the Queen of Ev and her children have been transformed into royal-purple ornaments, while the people from Oz have been changed into green ornaments. Dorothy enters the Nome King's palace to wind up Tiktok and make her own guesses, but Tiktok's final guess fails and Dorothy initially fails several times. She correctly identifies a purple kitten as Prince Evring, restoring him to human form, and returns with him to the throne room after failing to save any other members of the royal family of Ev.

15. Billina Frightens the Nome King

The Nome King tells the Scarecrow that the Tin Woodman has become a gold ornament and expects Dorothy to join his collection. Billina lays an egg under the Nome King's throne, frightening him because eggs are poisonous to Nomes, and uses it to secure permission to enter the enchanted palace and make guesses. Dorothy returns safely with Prince Evring after correctly guessing one enchantment, but the Scarecrow fails and becomes a solid gold card-receiver. Billina then enters the enchanted palace to make her own guesses, despite Dorothy's warning.

16. Purple, Green, and Gold

Billina searches the Nome King's underground palace and uses her knowledge to identify and reverse the transformations of the Queen of Ev and her nine children, the people of Oz, Ozma of Oz, the Scarecrow, and Tiktok. She discovers that Ozma was transformed into an emerald grasshopper and restores her after realizing the grasshopper was not alive. The Tin Woodman remains missing because no funny ornament can be identified as his transformation. When the group tries to leave and confront the Nome King, they find his motionless, heavily armed warriors filling the hall and the enraged King waiting on his throne.

17. The Scarecrow Wins the Fight

Billina's mission restores the transformed people of Ev and Oz, causing the bell above the Nome King's throne to ring repeatedly and enraging the Nome King. The Nome King summons his warriors and orders Dorothy seized, but the Lion, Hungry Tiger, and Sawhorse protect her. When Ozma's private soldier attacks the Nome warriors, the Scarecrow throws Billina's eggs at the Nome King, sending the warriors into a terrified escape. Billina then tells Dorothy to take the Nome King's jeweled belt.

18. The Fate of the Tin Woodman

Dorothy takes the Nome King's magic belt and uses it to open the passage and protect her companions from pursuing Nome warriors. Dorothy and the others cannot find the Tin Woodman, even after the Nome King identifies and searches for the ornament into which he transformed him. The group escapes the Nome King's palace and reaches the outer air, then passes the giant of iron when Dorothy commands the belt to stop him.

19. The King of Ev

Ozma, Dorothy, the Queen mother of Ev, and the royal children return to Ev after defeating the Nome King. Evring accidentally reveals that the Tin Woodman has been transformed into a tin whistle, and Billina restores him by touching the whistle and saying “Ev.” The people of Ev welcome the travelers, and the Queen mother crowns her eldest son King Evardo Fifteenth. The royal household celebrates, and Billina receives a necklace of pearls and sapphires.

20. The Emerald City

Dorothy accepts Ozma's invitation to return from Ev to the Land of Oz, crossing the desert with Ozma, Tiktok, Billina, the Scarecrow, the Tin Woodman, the Cowardly Lion, and others. They are welcomed throughout the Land of Oz and travel by yellow brick road to the Emerald City. At a grand reception in the royal palace, Dorothy is made a Princess of Oz, while several companions receive gifts and honors. During the feast, Ozma promotes the private Omby Amby to Captain General and Commander of her Body Guard, while the Hungry Tiger consumes all the food and then loses his appetite.

21. Dorothy's Magic Belt

Dorothy sees through Ozma's magic picture that Uncle Henry in Australia is ill and decides to return to him. Glinda the Good explains that the magic belt can transport Dorothy from fairyland but would be lost outside it, so Dorothy gives the belt to Ozma. Ozma agrees to use the belt to bring Dorothy back whenever Dorothy gives a signal. Dorothy says farewell to her friends in the Land of Oz and is transported to Uncle Henry, whom she promises to nurse.
